Frequently Asked Questions

How can I apply to become a designer with DVSY?

We welcome applications from talented designers around the world. You can submit your portfolio and information through our contact form or email us at collaborate@dvsy.com with samples of your work.

What is the typical response time for inquiries?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days. During peak periods, it may take up to 5 business days. Thank you for your patience!

Do you accept collaboration proposals?

Absolutely! We love collaborating with creative partners. Send us your proposal with details about your vision, and we'll review it. Email collaborate@dvsy.com for partnership opportunities.

How can I stay updated about new events and releases?

Subscribe to our newsletter through the website or follow us on social media (@DVSY) to stay in the loop about new collections, events, and exclusive announcements.

Are there job opportunities at DVSY?

Yes, we're always looking for talented individuals. Check our careers page or email careers@dvsy.com with your resume and cover letter. We hire for various roles including design, marketing, operations, and more.

How do you ensure sustainability in your designs?

Sustainability is core to our mission. We work with eco-friendly materials, support ethical production practices, and partner with designers committed to environmental responsibility. Learn more in our blog section.
